Routines are scheduled work on a bot you own. They fire in the owner's local weekday hours.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/frames/bot-computer.svg" alt="Computer rail where you pause and resume routines" />
</Frame>

## Set one up

1. Sign in and open a bot you own. Guests cannot create routines.
2. Add a routine for weekday work you want repeated.
3. Pause and resume from the Computer rail.

Hours follow **your** local weekday clock — not UTC on the guest, and not a Chat reminder.
