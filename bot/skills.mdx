## Enable a skill

1. Sign in. A guest cannot enable or run skills.
2. Open a bot you own.
3. Enable the first-party skills that bot should follow.

Skills are not Deep Research and not Code's workspace catalog. See [Bot tools](/bot/tools) for the `skill` tool, and [How Bot works](/bot/how-it-works) for what the user sees.
