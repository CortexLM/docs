The account's bots are listed once, in the shell sidebar. The pane is the *open* bot — conversation in the centre, Computer rail on the right.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/frames/bot-computer.svg" alt="Open bot with conversation and Computer rail" />
</Frame>

An account with no bots gets the setup form, not an empty list. Sprite, Finch and Pebble are not defaults.

Guests cannot create or drive a bot. Exact `/bot` is the public product page for a guest.
