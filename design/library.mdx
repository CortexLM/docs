The Design library holds components, pages, and tokens you reuse on a canvas.

<Note>
A real Design canvas screenshot will land with the staging PNG set. This page does not use a simplified SVG mock of the product.
</Note>

## Add an item

1. Open the canvas in the [Design desktop](/design/desktop) app.
2. Create a component, page, or token.
3. Add a thumbnail when you have one. Thumbnails are uploaded — they are not rasterised on this site.

Library items belong to your signed-in account. They are not Chat Library files. See [Chat Library](/chat/library).
