Cortex Chat is the conversation product at [cortex.foundation](https://cortex.foundation). Ask questions, write, research with citations, work with documents and images, and keep related work together in projects. Chat works before you sign in; projects, files, memory, plans, and connections belong to a signed-in account.

## Where Chat stops

Chat does not run Code's workspace shell or Bot's computer. Its Python tool is a snippet sandbox with no workspace and cannot see a repository. Use [Code](/code) for a repository, [Bot](/bot) for an always-on teammate with a desktop, and the [CLI](/cli) to drive Code from a terminal.
