Chat answers stream as they are written. You see tokens, then tools, then the finished reply — not a dump after the turn ends.

## What happens on a drop

If the connection drops, the product reconnects. Run state lives in the thread, not in a status line about the transport.

You do not need to resend the same turn to recover the reply that already started.
