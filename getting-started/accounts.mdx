---
title: "Accounts and sign-in"
description: "Sign in via the Cortex app."
---

Sign in at [cortex.foundation](https://cortex.foundation).

Chat works before you sign in. Projects, plugins, Code, Bot, and Design need a signed-in account.

The CLI and desktop app use the same account. Sign in from those apps when they ask — this site does not document the sign-in protocol.

Export your data or delete your account from **Settings → Data & privacy**.
