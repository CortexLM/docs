---
title: "What is Cortex"
description: "Four products, one account, one domain."
---

Cortex is four products that share an account at `cortex.foundation`:

| Product | What you open | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cortex Chat** | `/` | Everyone, including before you sign in |
| **Cortex Code** | `/code` | Signed-in members (others see the public page) |
| **Cortex Bot** | `/bot` | Signed-in members (others see the public page) |
| **Cortex Design** | Desktop app | Signed-in members |

Chat works unsigned. Code, Bot, plugins, and Design need a signed-in account so a grant you could never revoke cannot be created.

English is the source of truth for interface copy. The web app localizes it.
Product names stay untranslated.

Hostnames on the zone:

| Host | What |
| --- | --- |
| `cortex.foundation` | Web app and public pages |
| `software.cortex.foundation` | CLI installers |
| `releases.cortex.foundation` | Desktop updater feed |
| `docs.cortex.foundation` | This site |
