The product is **Cortex**. Open [cortex.foundation](https://cortex.foundation).

1. **Chat** works before you sign in. Pick Chat, type, send. Answers stream as they arrive.
2. **Sign in** when you want projects, plugins, Code, Bot, or Design. See [Accounts](/getting-started/accounts).
3. **Code** and **Bot** working UI are for signed-in accounts. Exact `/code` and `/bot` stay public product pages if you are not signed in.
4. **Desktop / CLI** if you need This PC, SSH, or Design's working UI. [Download Cortex](/getting-started/desktop).
