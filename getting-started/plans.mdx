---
title: "Plans and quotas"
description: "Free, Pro, Enterprise. Quotas fail closed."
---

Your plan and remaining quotas live on **Settings → Plan & billing** and in
the product when a limit is reached. Exhausting a window is `quota_exceeded`;
a capability the plan does not include at all is `entitlement_required`.

Do not invent a paywall in front of a quota. Cortex-Image-1 on Free is
`images_per_day`.

Until **2026-09-07T23:59:59Z** (inclusive), a signed-in member may claim one
month of Cortex Pro from the product. Claiming twice does not stack. Paying
Pro is left alone.
