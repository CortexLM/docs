Your plan and remaining quotas live on **Settings → Plan & billing** and in the product when a limit is reached.

## How limits fail

| What happened | Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You used the window | `quota_exceeded` | Try again when the window resets |
| The plan does not include it | `entitlement_required` | That capability is not on this plan |

Do not invent a paywall in front of a quota. Cortex-Image-1 on Free is `images_per_day`. See [Image generation](/chat/images) and [Errors](/api/errors).

## Plans

| Plan | Where you see it |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Default for a signed-in account |
| Pro | Settings → Plan & billing |
| Enterprise | Settings → Plan & billing |

Exact numbers live in the product. This page does not invent a price list.

## Closed Pro claim

Through **7 September 2026** (inclusive), a signed-in member could claim one month of Cortex Pro from the product. That window has closed. Claiming twice did not stack. Paying Pro is left alone.
